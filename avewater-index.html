Our Commitment to You

Áve is built on intentional moments. Every piece of information you share with us exists to deepen that intention — to connect you with music, with community, and with the experiences we are building for people like you.

We collect your name and phone number because we want to know who you are. Not as a data point, but as a person. The Áve Foundation is building programs that connect young people and underserved communities with clean water, wellness education, and music discovery. Your engagement with Áve, starting with this moment, is part of that story.

We will use your information to send you your playlist, to tell you when Áve is activating near you again, to invite you into experiences and events you would actually want to attend, and to connect you with the artists and communities that Áve is championing. We will never sell your data. We will never use it for anything that does not serve this mission.

You chose to be here. We take that seriously.

Who We Are
Áve Water LLC, a California limited liability company. Contact: ave@avewater.org · avewater.org
What We Collect
Your name and phone number (required). Your email address (optional). Your mood selection, music platform preference, activation location type, and the date and time of your interaction.
SMS Communications — TCPA Disclosure
By providing your phone number and checking the consent box, you expressly consent to receive recurring automated and non-automated text messages from Áve Water LLC at the number provided, including playlist confirmations, activation announcements, event invitations, and promotional messages. Message frequency will vary. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or participation. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. For help, reply HELP or contact ave@avewater.org.
How We Use Your Information
To deliver your playlist. To notify you when Áve activates near you. To invite you to events, experiences, and Foundation programming. To improve our activations and understand engagement. To support the Áve Foundation's mission of connecting communities with clean water, wellness education, and music discovery. We do not sell your personal information to third parties under any circumstances.
Data Sharing
We share your information only with trusted service providers who help us operate our platform, under strict confidentiality agreements. We may disclose information if required by law.
Your Rights
You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting ave@avewater.org. California residents have additional rights under the CCPA. To exercise any right, contact ave@avewater.org.
Opt-Out
SMS: Reply STOP to any message at any time. Email: Contact ave@avewater.org. Opting out will not affect your ability to participate in future Áve activations.
