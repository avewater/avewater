Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Áve Water LLC ("Áve," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you interact with an Áve activation, redeem a gift, or vote for a song, we may collect:

  • Your name and phone number, when you provide them
  • Mood, song, or preference selections you make during an activation
  • The location or event where you interacted with Áve

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Deliver the gift, playlist, or content you requested
  • Send transactional SMS messages confirming your request (for example, delivering a redeemed gift or playlist link)
  • Send marketing SMS messages about upcoming Áve activations and events, only if you have separately opted in
  • Understand which activations and experiences resonate with our community, in aggregate

SMS Communications

If you provide your phone number and consent to receive text messages, Áve will send you messages related to the specific request you made (such as delivering a gift or playlist). If you separately opt in to marketing messages, you may also receive occasional messages about future Áve activations and events, up to 4 per month.

Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of any SMS program at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance. Consent to receive SMS messages is never a condition of purchase or participation in an Áve activation.

Data Sharing

Áve does not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your phone number or name with partner venues or brands without your knowledge — data collected at a partner activation is used by Áve for the purposes described in this policy.

Data Retention

We retain the information you provide for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law.

Your Rights

California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your information, and opt out of marketing communications at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to ave@avewater.org.
