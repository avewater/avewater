Áve Foundation

Beyond the cup.

The Áve Foundation looks after the community that shows up.

We bring hydration, wellness and music programs to neighborhoods, universities and families. Clean, cold water and a few minutes of music, offered to everyone who comes.

Venue partners help fund every program. The Foundation is forming as a 501(c)(3).

Who we look after

  • Neighborhoods. Water, wellness and music at community events, open to all.
  • Universities. Campus partnerships and student-led activations that care for students between classes and games.
  • Families. Programs where kids and parents can slow down together.

Work with us

Community groups, schools and venue partners can write to ave@avewater.org.
