Booking terms

Last updated November 2026

These terms apply when a business books the Áve Venue Package through avewater.org. "Áve," "we" and "us" mean Áve Water LLC. "You" means the business making the booking. Our general Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply.

What is included

The package price covers the infused water and bottles listed on the booking page, delivery inside our service area (any travel fee is shown before you pay), setup before your start time and pickup after your end time. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.

Booking and payment

  • Bookings need at least 48 hours notice before the event start time, or 72 hours in Los Angeles, Ventura and Santa Barbara counties.
  • Some areas carry a travel fee. If one applies, it is shown as its own line before you pay.
  • We accept a limited number of bookings per day. A date is held for you for about 30 minutes while you complete checkout.
  • Payment is taken in full at booking through Stripe. Your booking is confirmed once the payment goes through, and you will receive an email receipt.

Dispensers

  • If we bring dispensers and you choose pickup, they remain Áve property and we collect them after your event at no charge. Please keep them somewhere safe and reachable at pickup time.
  • If you choose to keep a dispenser, the keep price per dispenser is added at checkout and the dispenser is yours.
  • If a dispenser we are scheduled to pick up is missing or damaged beyond normal use, we may charge the keep price for that dispenser.

Cancellations and changes

  • Cancel 72 or more hours before your event start time for a full refund.
  • Cancel less than 72 hours before your event start time for a 50% refund.
  • To cancel or change a booking, email ave@avewater.org with your booking ID. The time we receive your email is the time we use. Date and time changes depend on availability.
  • If we have to cancel for any reason, you get a full refund.

Your venue

Please give us accurate setup notes, safe access to the setup area at your start time and a contact on site.

Text messages

If you agree at checkout, Áve Water LLC will text you about your booking. Marketing texts are sent only if you separately opt in. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.
