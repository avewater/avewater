What We Are

This is not
a beverage.

Áve is an experience layer integrated into the physical spaces where people move, breathe, connect, and buy. Water is our entry point. Curated music is our medium. Presence, that rare moment when your environment feels intentional, is our product.


We partner with the brands our customers already trust to create moments they remember long after they leave. Every activation captures verified consumer data tied to a real purchase, creating an intelligence platform no competitor has built.

"Water is the entry point. Music is the medium. Presence is the product."

Founded in San Diego, CA. Operating across fitness, retail, and hospitality. Built as a subsidiary of Mxkey Music Holdings LLC. A for-profit experience platform with a nonprofit mission arm.

Áve Foundation

Beyond
the cup.

Presence is not a luxury reserved for those who can afford it. The Áve Foundation extends our mission into communities, universities, and institutions, bringing intentional hydration, wellness programming, and student-led activations to those who need it most.


Every commercial partnership funds our mission. The for-profit entity builds the brand. The Foundation attracts grant capital and deploys it where it matters.
